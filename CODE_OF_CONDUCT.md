# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of identity,
experience, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Critique evidence, interfaces, and methodology rather than people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish a software audit from a scientific judgment.
- Respect confidentiality, copyright, licenses, and research participants.
- Accept correction, explain disagreement, and keep discussions relevant.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate misrepresentation,
or publishing confidential review material is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's security contact.
The maintainer may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
